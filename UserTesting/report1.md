
### Participant background
The user was a friend in course 12 who has not used Spaced Repetition software. They consented to perform some tasks with the app understanding that I would ask for their thought process in performing the tasks and intervene when necessary. Algorithm-specific context in the instructions was translated into their current courses or replaced with dummy text. 

#### Making points and topics
They filled out the point’s text and the topic’s text in their respective search bars and pressed enter. The interface hid every point in the home screen and a point wasn’t created. They paused, articulating that the placeholder text suggested they could immediately put a topic to a point, and didn’t know what to do next, an execution gulf.I intervened, explaining that the app’s workflow forces making points and topics separately. Succeeded after intervention.

#### Making prompts
They tried to make a prompt by going to the prompts tab, but only saw the prepopulated prompt and no way to create one. I intervened by asking where they’ve seen prompts in the ui before. They then selected some points and tried to use the add to prompt button to generate a new prompt. Execution gulf: unlike points and topics, the prompts tab has no create action and its hint text appears only if no prompts exist. Succeeded making a prompt after intervention.

#### Using shared prompts
They informed me that sharing was so slow it was ‘ragebait’, but succeeded. 

#### Scheduling
They were able to find the scheduled prompt and perform a check on it, but they didn’t know what counted as ‘mentioning’ and how the number of points mentioned would affect the due date. Succeeded at the task, but there was a gulf in evaluation since nothing in the interface showed how a check’s outcome changed the due date.
